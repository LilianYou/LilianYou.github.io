I am an [Instructor](https://connects.catalyst.harvard.edu/Profiles/display/Person/207457) at [McLean Hospital](https://www.mcleanhospital.org/) and [Harvard Medical School](https://hms.harvard.edu/) (I go by Lily). My research develops computational approaches to study brain–behavior relationships under real-world heterogeneity, with a focus on aging, neuropsychiatric symptoms, and Alzheimer's disease and related dementias (ADRD).

At McLean, I work with [Prof. Lisa D. Nickerson](https://www.mcleanhospital.org/profile/lisa-nickerson) in the [Applied Neuroimaging Statistics Research (ANSR) Lab](https://www.mcleanmri.org/ansl.html) at the McLean Imaging Center and [Prof. David Harper](https://www.mcleanhospital.org/profile/david-harper) in the Division of Geriatric Psychiatry. My work develops and applies machine learning and multimodal data fusion—including [SuperBigFLICA](https://github.com/ANSR-laboratory/SuperBigFLICA_McL)—across large, multi-cohort datasets spanning structural/functional neuroimaging, fluid biomarkers, electronic health records (EHR), and text-derived phenotypes. I conceptualize this direction as *computational neuroecology*: modeling brain–behavior relationships in the complex, heterogeneous settings where clinical outcomes emerge. This work is supported in part by an [Alzheimer's Association Research Fellowship](/funding/) (2026–2029).

My research trajectory bridges cognitive neuroscience and clinical data science. I completed my Ph.D. in Cognitive Sciences at UC Irvine with [Prof. Elizabeth R. Chrastil](https://faculty.sites.uci.edu/spatialneuro/), investigating the neural and computational mechanisms of human spatial navigation. As a postdoctoral fellow at Massachusetts General Hospital with [Prof. Sudeshna Das](https://researchers.mgh.harvard.edu/profile/11948449/Sudeshna-Das), I applied deep learning and NLP methods to heterogeneous EHR and clinical data in dementia. Across these areas, my work is grounded in an interdisciplinary foundation spanning experimental psychology, cognitive neuroscience, geography, and computational modeling.

I also mentor Harvard-affiliated students on computational research projects within the ANSR Lab; see [Mentoring & Teaching](/teaching/) for current opportunities.
